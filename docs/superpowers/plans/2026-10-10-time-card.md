# Time card and calmer status marks (visual authority, part 3) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans (inline) to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace the time card with one that shows the time of day as a landscape (sky colour by phase, sun or moon on a day arc, sunrise and sunset), rolling digits for the time, and a 60-minute reliability strip; and replace the coloured status pills with a dot and text everywhere except the AQI category.

**Architecture:** Pure functions decide everything (day phase and luminary position from `suncalc` times, reliability buckets from the loaded history); the component only draws them. The clock is injectable so every phase can be reviewed. Sky colours are tokens per theme with a contrast test.

**Tech Stack:** React 19, `suncalc`, `@number-flow/react`, framer-motion (already installed), Tailwind v4, Vitest, Playwright.

**Spec:** the option page agreed in chat on 2026-10-10 (B "Солнце" + C "Табло" + D "Лента надёжности"; pills only for AQI).

## Global Constraints

- Station location and zone are constants: Tashkent time (`Asia/Tashkent`, UTC+5), coordinates of the university campus (41.343 N, 69.205 E). The time shown is the station's, not the visitor's.
- Phases: `night`, `dawn`, `morning`, `day`, `evening`, `dusk`. From the `suncalc` times: dawn starts at `dawn`, morning at `sunrise`, day at `goldenHourEnd`, evening at `goldenHour`, dusk at `sunset`, night at `dusk`; the next night ends at the following `dawn`.
- Arc: from sunrise to sunset the sun moves along it; from sunset to the next sunrise the moon moves along the same arc (phase drawn from `getMoonIllumination`).
- Reliability strip: 60 one-minute buckets ending at the current minute; `ok` = at least 6 readings (half of the 12 a healthy device sends), `partial` = 1–5, `gap` = none.
- Status marks: a dot in the status colour plus text; no border, no fill. The AQI category badge stays as it is.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (digits and the pulsing dot do not animate).
- New texts in ru, uz and en. `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint` (0 errors), `pnpm test`, `pnpm build` pass after every task. Commits end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Sonnet 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

## Review Focus

- Phase boundaries around midnight and across the date change (night spans two calendar days; the arc position must not jump).
- Invalid `suncalc` output (NaN times) must fall back to a neutral state, not crash or render NaN in SVG.
- History with a hole at the very start or end of the hour, a device that sends faster than every 5 s, and an empty history (offline).
- Rolling minutes at 59 → 00 and the hour change (digits roll upward, no reverse animation).
- Sky tints under the text in every phase and theme (text stays at 4.5:1).
- Visitor in another time zone sees Tashkent time, labelled.
